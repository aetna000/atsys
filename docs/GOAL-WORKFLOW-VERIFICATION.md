## What now happens

Invoking `$atsys <objective>` uses the installed coding-agent intake skill to inspect the request/project, extract goal, deliverables, success criteria and constraints, record routine assumptions, and clarify material unanswered questions. It builds an ordered plan with purpose-bound observers/hooks and bounded recovery. Schema/launch guards reject an incomplete declared contract or unresolved material decisions; legacy direct operator configurations remain compatible and are labelled legacy.

Execution shows goal stages with provenance, independent of heartbeats/tool traffic. Terminal states automatically persist a readable, digest-addressed report covering goal, attempts/recovery, acceptance evidence, domain outcomes, remaining failures/limitations, available costs and next action. Report failure is visible and fences completion. Domain results remain separately sourced; full progress or accepted workflow does not mean every domain task passed. No extra model call generates a report. Semantic intake judgment is performed by the coding agent; the service cannot prove that a natural-language success criterion is sufficient.
